# LERSAIS: scholarly research institute

Reference direction: contemporary academic editorial design on a white canvas. Retain the LERSAIS identity and the existing University of Pittsburgh SVG wordmark.

## Visual specification
The content canvas is white with ink #121a27, muted text #5e6672, hairlines #dfe3e8, Pitt Royal Blue #003594, deep blue #001f4e, and Pitt Gold #ffb81c. There are no background photographs, illustrations, or generated artwork. Major headings use an editorial system-serif stack: Iowan Old Style, Palatino Linotype, Palatino, Georgia, serif. Body copy and interface controls use Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif. Headlines are restrained and left aligned; small uppercase section labels, ordinal numbers, and thin rules create a research-publication rhythm.

Liquid-glass effects are limited to structural interface regions. The sticky header, desktop navigation, research jump links, and filter toolbars use translucent white panels with pale blue-to-gold gradients, subtle blur, fine borders, and very light shadows. Main content and cards stay flat. Primary actions are compact Pitt-blue rectangles with 7px corners; secondary actions are text-and-arrow links. The footer uses a deep Pitt-blue gradient with a subtle gold glow and a frosted top rule.

## Layout and behavior
Main gutters scale from 24px to 56px and content is capped at 1360px. Research is the single lab homepage. A slim Pitt Gold conference announcement sits above the shared glass header. The page then follows an institutional editorial sequence: a two-column mission hero, three flat News & events rows, a light blue-gray full-width research band with three numbered columns, a deep Pitt-blue community band with the real LERSAIS team photograph, and a concise Research inquiries section. The mission hero pairs the research focus with the full laboratory name, research summary, and People/Grants links. Each research column contains the complete existing description, a compact topic list, and researcher links. Do not duplicate an overview card grid above the research details. Inner pages pair compact serif headings with flat rows and glass filter bars.

At 900px and below, navigation becomes a disclosure menu and the homepage hero becomes one column. At 640px and below, grids become single columns, filter tabs scroll horizontally when needed, and footer columns collapse. All site images use object-fit: scale-down inside stable frames so photographs remain uncropped and low-resolution assets are not enlarged. JavaScript enhances filters, profile disclosures, the gallery dialog, and the menu; content remains readable without JavaScript.

## Content and routes
Homepage search has been removed. People and grants retain local page-level filters and search. Routes are research, people, grants, news, teaching, gallery, and contact. The root and index.html redirect to research.html, preserving query/hash with JavaScript and providing a no-JavaScript fallback. Header and footer wordmarks link directly to research.html. Publications redirects to grants. Preserve the existing member biographies, current student years, alumni placement, grant data, source links, and repository images. Do not invent people, metrics, dates, grants, or research claims.
