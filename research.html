Trustworthy AI

Understanding and strengthening privacy, trust, and security in machine learning.

Our researchers explore privacy in machine learning and AI, differential privacy, and privacy-preserving machine learning. The work brings together questions of data protection, trust, and the security of intelligent systems.

  • Privacy-preserving machine learning
  • Differential privacy
  • Trust and security in AI

Privacy-preserving systems

Protecting data across distributed, cloud, and emerging computing environments.

We study privacy-conscious computation and data sharing in untrusted and outsourced environments, including cloud and edge computing, distributed systems, blockchains, and extended reality.

  • Distributed systems & data sharing
  • Cloud, edge & blockchain
  • Privacy in emerging environments

Security & access control

Developing the foundations of resilient systems and secure information access.

Our interests include attribute-based access control, insider threats, security and privacy in IoT, and secure health information exchange. We connect foundational security questions with the systems people depend on.

  • Attribute-based access control
  • IoT & network security
  • Secure information exchange
